Cut through the clutter and find what you need faster. Sort, filter, and search every crafting and cooking menu by category, biome, and availability. Works at every crafting station, including cauldrons and ketills, and tons of other modded stations. Look out, Marie Kondo.


---

## Features

- **Sort by:**
  - **Alphabetical**
  - **Category:** (list and filter out specific tools, materials, weapons and armor, or by food and mead types)
  - **Craftability:** (if you've got the materials on hand already, reorder to show all current craftables up top)
  - **Biome:** (reorder all craftables by the biome that unlocks the recipe)
- **Filter out what you don't need.** Hide the materials and categories you never make.
- **Live search** can narrow down the list as you type.
- **Works on all crafting stations:** every crafting and cooking station, modded station, and across most popular mods.
- **Client-side**, so you can install this without needing to sync with the server.

- a horizontal bar with two clusters of buttons: 
	- four main sort buttons (left)

- a search bar above the crafting window
- a vertical column of category filter buttons.

## Other features

- **Click and drag** works on the biome buttons and category buttons too for bulk toggle on/off states.
- **Primary buttons combine** and sort in the following order: 1) biome, 2) craftables, 3) category, 4) alphabetical.
- **Saved per station:** meaning that your choices, decisions to hide early-game biome foods, and/or hatred for spears carries over across sessions.

## Modded items

Vanilla biomes and categories come from the [Valheim Wiki](https://valheim.weirdgloop.org/). Items the wiki doesn't cover, including items from other mods, follow the same rules:

- **Craftable items** take the latest biome among their ingredients. A sword made with Black Metal is a Plains item, available only after you unlock black metal.
- **Raw materials** take the earliest biome they spawn or drop in.
- **Categories** come from the item's type, weapon skill, armor slot or food stats.

Any item can be moved to a different biome with an override (see Configuration).

### Overrides

To move an item to a different biome, add a line to `BepInEx/config/CraftingAndCookingDecluttered.overrides.txt`:

```ini
# PrefabName = Biome
SwordIron = Swamp
```

Use the item's prefab name, listed in the dump file. Lines starting with `#` are ignored. Biomes: Meadows, BlackForest, Ocean, Swamp, Mountain, Plains, Mistlands, AshLands, DeepNorth.

## Compatibility

Client-side only. Other players and the server don't need it.

Tested alongside the following mods:

- [AzuExtendedPlayerInventory](https://thunderstore.io/c/valheim/p/Azumatt/AzuExtendedPlayerInventory/) by Azumatt
- [AzuCraftyBoxes](https://thunderstore.io/c/valheim/p/Azumatt/AzuCraftyBoxes/) by Azumatt
- [Epic Loot](https://thunderstore.io/c/valheim/p/RandyKnapp/EpicLoot/) by RandyKnapp
- [Warfare](https://thunderstore.io/c/valheim/p/Therzie/Warfare/), [Armory](https://thunderstore.io/c/valheim/p/Therzie/Armory/), [Wizardry](https://thunderstore.io/c/valheim/p/Therzie/Wizardry/) and [Monstrum](https://thunderstore.io/c/valheim/p/Therzie/Monstrum/) by Therzie
- [MultiCraft](https://thunderstore.io/c/valheim/p/MaxiMods/MultiCraft/) by MaxiMods
- [Quick Stack Store Sort Trash Restock](https://thunderstore.io/c/valheim/p/Goldenrevolver/Quick_Stack_Store_Sort_Trash_Restock/) by Goldenrevolver

Other mods that sort or filter the crafting menu are unlikely to work alongside this one.

If a UI mod moves the crafting panel, the toolbar should follow it. Use offset settings in the advanced setting panel to fine-tune the position.

## Installation

**Mod manager:** install with r2modman, Gale or Thunderstore Mod Manager.

**Manual:** install [BepInExPack Valheim](https://thunderstore.io/c/valheim/p/denikson/BepInExPack_Valheim/), then put `CraftingAndCookingDecluttered.dll` in `BepInEx/plugins`.
